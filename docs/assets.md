# Assets and reuse

The 20 product WebP illustrations and fictional product records were created for this independent demo using AI-assisted asset creation and local optimization. They are illustrations, not verified product photographs. Original editorial SVG compositions are self-contained, with no external image links, copied Isha marks or borrowed product descriptions. Reference design exports and their unlicensed images are excluded.

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are self-hosted Latin variable WOFF2 fonts under SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full copyright and license notices remain alongside each font in public/fonts/. Source licenses: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond and https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope. Original asset authorship does not grant rights to third-party design references or imply any affiliation. No general open-source license is asserted beyond the included third-party license notices; publication visibility is not itself a license grant.

The Stillness Edit adds two original manually authored SVG assets: public/editorial/stillness-mountains.svg (imagined indigo mountain horizon and crescent) and public/editorial/stillness-study.svg (abstract stone, vessel and stem). They use self-contained vector primitives and do not reuse supplied reference-image bytes, recognizable third-party sculpture or branding.
